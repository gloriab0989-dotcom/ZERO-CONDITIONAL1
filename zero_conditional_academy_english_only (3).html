What is the Zero Conditional?

We use the Zero Conditional for facts, rules, routines, and results that are generally true. In a teaching context, it can explain what usually happens when teachers or students do something.

IF + present simple, present simple
Remember: Use the present simple in both parts of the sentence. You can put the if-clause first or second. When the sentence starts with if, use a comma.
📝
Classroom routine

If teachers give clear instructions, students understand the task.

🗣️
Learning advice

If students practise every day, they improve their English.

💛
Positive classroom

If a teacher listens carefully, students feel respected.

Examples in a Teaching Context

Read these examples aloud. Notice that both verbs use the present simple.

🗓️Advice

If you plan your lesson, you use class time well.

🛡️Classroom management

If students know the rules, they work more safely.

💬Feedback

If teachers give helpful feedback, learners understand their mistakes.

🙋Participation

If teachers ask open questions, students share more ideas.

🎧English practice

If learners speak English regularly, they build confidence.

🤝Inclusion

If teachers respect different needs, more students feel included.

Quick tip: With he, she, it, add -s or -es to most present simple verbs: the student learns, the teacher watches.
